# ABSA_Customer_Insight_project
Why the youth leave ABSA for Capitec and TymeBank - Excel comparison of fees, queues and service, Nelspruit reviews. 

## Objective
Compare ABSA vs Capitec vs TymeBank to understand youth switching in Nelspruit

## My Table
|Bank | Monthly fee| Branch needed? | Opening Time|
|ABSA | R69 -R255 |Yes, long queue | Branch |
|Capitec | R7.50 -R35 | No, App | 3 mins app |
|TymeBank | R0.00 | No, Boxer/PnP | 5 mins at till |
Youth sees: R255 + 1 hour queue vs R7.50 + 3 mins on phone vs R0 + 5 mins buying bread at till

## Analysed Nelspruit Reviews
- Long queue, system slow
- Not friendly service
- waited 1 hour, not assisted
Findings: Queue + service is a bigger problem than fees

## Recommendations for ABSA
ABSA Nelspruit needs more staff at peak times to reduce queues
Fix system speed (WiFi)
